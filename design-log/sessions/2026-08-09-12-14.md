### Key Decisions Made This Session
1. **Prefer Holded's `total` over recomputing from `price`, with a fallback**: chosen because it's the minimal, precise fix — reads the value Holded itself already computed correctly, instead of trying to out-guess Holded's internal precision. The fallback preserves all existing behavior when `total` isn't available or WC isn't tax-inclusive, so no regression risk for other setups.
2. **Did not implement retry/delay for `total` staleness**: hora-app flagged that `total` can lag a few seconds after a write, but that's a timing concern, not what this bug was about — added as a note in FIXED.md/LEARNED.md instead of scope creep into the fix.
